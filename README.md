# Voice Bounce

A TikTok-style camera effect prototype: control the paddle with your voice and keep the bouncing faces out of the water.

**Play:** https://hannahzz000.github.io/voice-bounce/ (open on a phone in Safari or Chrome, allow camera and microphone)

- **Voice Bounce** (black icon): higher pitch moves the paddle right, lower pitch moves it left.
- **Sing Happy Birthday** (pink icon, swipe right on the effect carousel): sing along and use the pitch of each word to catch the faces on the beat. Headphones recommended.
